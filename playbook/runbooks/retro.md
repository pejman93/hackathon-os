# Retro

Run it 2 to 4 weeks after the event.

## 1. Figures, from the records only

From the sheets, forms and the event log, never estimated: applications, places, started, presented,
teams, judge scores, audience votes, mentors and sessions, reviews. Each figure names its source.
Compare with the previous edition in one table.

## 2. Keep and change

Draft both lists from the figures and the log, each point with its source. Nobody is criticised or
named on slides.

## 3. Partner reports

Send a short report to each partner within 2 weeks.

## 4. Update the playbook

- [`traps.md`](../traps.md): anything that went wrong or nearly did.
- [`README.md`](../README.md): turn each lesson into a task at the right week.
- `brand/brand.json`: only if the look changed for good.
