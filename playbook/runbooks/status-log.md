# Status log

Keep one running log per event, in `STATUS.md`, in a private place.

## When

After every step that sends, decides or builds something.

## How

1. Set "Last updated" to today's date.
2. Put the entry in the right section: key facts, decisions, who got what, files, open to-dos.
3. Say what happened, when, with which account, and where the result is. Facts carry their source: a
   person and date, a file, or an email.
4. If a plan changed, mark the old line "replaced on <date>" and add the new one.
5. Keep the open to-dos numbered and current.

## Never write

Chat invite links, API keys, tokens, passwords, or participants' contact details.
